About the Philippine Peso

  • Code / symbol: PHP / ₱ (ISO 4217 code 608)
  • Central bank: Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP)
  • Subunit: 1 peso = 100 centavos (sentimo)
  • Coins: 1, 5, 10, 20 pesos (and 1, 5, 25 sentimo coins)
  • Banknotes: ₱20, ₱50, ₱100, ₱200, ₱500, ₱1,000
  • Regime: Managed floating rate

Tips for exchanging

  • Rates shown are mid-market; banks, airports and remittance centers add a spread, so expect a less favorable rate.
  • Money changers in Manila (e.g. Makati and Ermita) often beat airport and hotel rates.
  • Remittance services and bank fees vary – compare the total received, not just the headline rate.
  • Bring clean, undamaged USD notes; larger denominations usually get a better rate.
